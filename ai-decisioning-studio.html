The brief

One place for every AI decision

Lifecycle marketing has long run on rules: a segment per audience, a campaign per segment, and the offer, channel and send time picked by hand. With Unified AI Decisioning the marketer sets the goal, the creative and the guardrails. AI makes those choices for each customer, across every campaign in a Strategy, and learns from every result what works. The goal was one place to see all of it: what AI decided, what it changed, and who for.

The challenges

Know your customers. Control your AI. See what it will do.

Product

Optimization was scattered across tools

The challengeSetup lived in one place, reports in another and analytics in a third, with every agent chasing its own metric. Marketers couldn’t tell where to switch an agent on, or what it would bring.

The solutionOne Strategy holds the goal, the customer pool and every campaign. An optimization score shows where AI isn’t on yet, and each agent’s results sit beside its setup.

UX

Thousands of decisions, nothing to inspect

The challengeAI picks a promotion, a channel and an hour for each customer, so the combinations run into the thousands and a variation number says nothing. A distribution shows what happened, but a marketer who has handed decisions to AI also needs to know why.

The solutionTransparency: the why and the who. Every decision says why AI chose it, measured against a random control group, and customers AI treats alike are grouped into profiles that show who they are, how they behave, and which promotion, channel and hour worked for them.

Trust

Large brands hesitate to switch AI on

The challengeLarge e-commerce brands hold back from handing campaigns to AI. A black box gives them nothing to check.

The solutionSetup shows what AI will do before it’s switched on, and every result is measured against a random control group, so the brand sees what it did and what it brought.
