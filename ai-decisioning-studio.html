Optimization Power AI agents pulling in one direction

A connected workspace for marketers to set a Strategy’s goal, give AI clear boundaries and understand what it contributes across their campaigns.

The brief

Aligning AI agents to one goal

Optimove’s AI agents could already decide a lot for each customer: which campaign, which offer, which hour, which content. But turning an agent on happened in one place, its report lived in another, and its analytics in a third. Many users didn’t know where to activate an agent, what it would bring them, or how to get started with it.

Each agent also had its own measure: Send Time chased opens, Content chased clicks. Nothing tied them to the business objective the marketer actually owned. So the questions that mattered had no answer on any screen: is my objective moving, what did AI add to it, and where should I act next?

AI Decisioning Studio brings it all into one place: find the agents, see what each one brings, switch them on and read their results, all toward one goal.

What we saw

One objective across campaigns

In a customer walkthrough, a marketer who runs Optimove alone described the month: one business objective, 10 to 15 campaigns built to serve it, the same action reused in each so they read as a set. To see whether the objective moved, they opened every campaign, one at a time.

The goal

Shared goal for the agents

Give that objective a home. A Strategy groups the campaigns behind one primary KPI, so every AI agent optimizes toward the same goal, and the result is read once, for the whole Strategy, before anyone opens a campaign.

The feedback

Added value vs uplift

The existing reports showed uplift but never the total, so a good percentage could hide a small result. And one campaign rarely converts one way: a cross-sell can land as a renewal, an upgrade or an add-on. So the Strategy shows the increase beside its total and its figure per customer, with a secondary KPI for the other ways the goal is met.

Discovery & definition

Planning and review

The customer walkthrough showed the gap: marketers could review campaigns one by one, but not what they added up to together.

01 · Customer walkthrough

Cross-campaign goal

The marketer wanted total conversions and the impact each campaign added, in one place. The shared objective became the starting point of the workspace.

02 · Product alignment

Goal and boundaries

A Strategy sets one primary KPI and, optionally, a secondary KPI it also optimizes for. Its customer pool defines who the Strategy serves, so audience size and reach belong on the overview.

03 · Structure and review

Setup and review together

Campaign work moved from a modal to a full page, with room for setup, results and explanations. The campaign context never leaves the screen.

UX decisions & flows

Strategy and campaign structure

Two flows: read the Strategy, then let AI optimize each campaign within limits the marketer sets.

Structural decisions

Wireframes reconstructed for this case study.

01 · Strategy lobby

Start from the goals

Every Strategy gets a card with its increase, AI uplift, customers and score, so the marketer picks where to look by outcome.

02 · Strategy overview

Put coverage before campaigns

Show who the Strategy is missing before anyone opens a campaign.

03 · Campaign workspace

Connect results and decisions

Every result sits beside the choices made for each customer, and the header shows each campaign’s share of the Strategy.

The strategy page

Strategy overview

One overview replaces the campaign-by-campaign check. The optimization score, the customer pool and the KPI results sit above the campaigns, so marketers see where the Strategy needs work and which campaign to open next.

Audience

Customer pool

The pool defines who the Strategy serves. One audience sits behind every number on the page.

Coverage

Reach by step

A funnel follows the pool to targeted and responded, with the share of targeted customers who responded. The gap points to the campaign to improve, or the one to add.

Measurement

Total value vs increase

Total KPI shows activity. The increase shows what the Strategy added, for the primary and the secondary KPI, each with its figure per customer. Every campaign’s share adds up to the Strategy’s number.

The connected workspace

Goal to Strategy view

From the Strategy lobby into one Strategy: its score, its customers, its KPIs, then the campaign that needs work.

The campaign page

Explainability

Five AI agents spend margin and decide what every customer receives. Marketers won’t hand that to a black box, so transparency became the core of this page.

Everything AI did on a campaign now sits in one place, the Explainability section: its outcomes, its results and every decision behind them.
