About

I'm a senior product designer and design lead with 10+ years across B2B and B2C products.

I own complex product experiences end to end: from user needs and business goals to concepts, flows, prototypes and the interfaces that ship.

I build design-system foundations and AI-powered workflows that help whole teams explore faster and stay consistent and on-brand.
